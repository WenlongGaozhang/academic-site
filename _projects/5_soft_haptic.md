As Co-Investigator and systems-engineering lead on the "SoftSensation" project, led development of a soft, hand-worn haptic force-feedback system used in a neuropathy-diagnosis clinical trial at UCL Hospitals, iterating on its mechanical structure and control algorithm. The work was covered by The Times, Sky News, and the Financial Times.
